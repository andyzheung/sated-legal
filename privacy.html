一、我们是谁

病友饭卡（英文名 Sated，以下简称"本应用"）是一款本机优先的饮食记录和嘌呤摄入参考工具。

  • 数据控制者：张文博（个人开发者）
  • 联系邮箱：andyzheung@aliyun.com
  • 支持页面：https://andyzheung.github.io/sated-legal/support.html
  • 隐私政策：https://andyzheung.github.io/sated-legal/privacy.html

二、我们处理哪些数据

本版本默认全部在本机处理以下数据：

  • 健康档案：每日参考目标、管理模式、肾功能状态选项。用于显示本地嘌呤预算和余额。
  • 饮食记录：餐次、食物、份量、估算嘌呤值、记录时间。用于生成今日汇总、历史记录和分享图片。
  • App 偏好：本机同意记录、引导完成状态（保存在本机 UserDefaults）。
  • 试用状态：试用开始与到期时间（保存在本机）。清除饮食数据不会重新开始试用。
  • 购买状态：App Store 返回的订阅或内购权益。用于解锁已购买功能。
  • 分享图片：你主动生成的饮食摘要图片。仅在你选择保存时写入照片图库。

本版本不提供 App 账号，不要求登录，也不会把饮食记录或健康档案上传到开发者的服务器。

三、照片和相机

本版本不提供菜品照片识别或菜单扫描。只有当你主动选择保存分享图片时，系统才会请求"仅添加照片"权限；本应用不会读取你的照片图库。

四、Apple 购买

订阅和内购由 Apple 处理。我们不会接收你的完整银行卡号或 Apple ID 密码。本应用只读取 Apple 提供的购买权益，用于解锁个人版或终身版权益。

删除本机数据不会取消 Apple 订阅。你可以在 iOS 系统的订阅管理页面取消或管理订阅。

五、我们不会做什么

  • 不出售健康数据。
  • 不使用广告或跟踪。
  • 不使用第三方分析 SDK。
  • 不把饮食记录或健康档案上传到开发者服务器。
  • 不提供 AI 识别、菜单扫描、家庭同步、家庭成员共享或通知提醒（当前版本）。
  • 不提供医学诊断、治疗、处方或风险预测。

六、系统备份和用户分享

本机数据可能受 iOS 系统备份、设备迁移或你主动导出的影响。你保存到照片图库、发送给他人或分享到其他 App 的图片，由对应的系统功能或第三方服务处理。

七、删除数据

你可以在本应用内进入设置 > 隐私与数据 > 删除本机数据，删除本机健康档案和饮食记录。该操作不会删除食物参考库，也不会取消 Apple 订阅。

如果未来版本提供服务端账号、AI 上传或家庭同步，我们会在隐私政策中补充对应的数据删除方式。

八、保留期限

健康档案和饮食记录默认保留在本机，直到你在 App 内删除、卸载本应用或系统清除数据。购买权益和订阅记录由 Apple 按其政策处理。本应用不会把健康记录发送给开发者服务器。

九、健康内容说明

本应用中的嘌呤数值、每日目标和余额展示只用于饮食记录和一般健康参考。食物嘌呤会因食材品种、部位、加工方式、烹饪和份量而变化。本应用不提供诊断、治疗、处方或风险预测；痛风急性发作、慢性肾病或其他合并症用户应遵循医生或营养师建议。

本版本参考库选用 USDA and ODS-NIH Database for the Purine Content of Foods Release 2.0 (2025) 中由 USDA 检测的美国食品样品。总嘌呤采用四种嘌呤碱基总量，不包含另列的尿酸值；数据针对明确的生熟和处理状态，覆盖有限。来源和方法可在 App 内查看。

十、未成年人

本应用面向成年人健康记录场景。我们不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

十一、政策更新

如果未来加入 AI 识别、菜单扫描、家庭同步、账号、通知或云服务，我们会先更新本政策，并在应用内说明新增的数据处理方式。

十二、如何联系我们

如需就个人信息处理提出查询、更正、删除、撤回同意、投诉或其他请求，请发送邮件至：andyzheung@aliyun.com。我们会在核实请求后依照适用法律处理。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

1. Who We Are

Sated (Chinese name 病友饭卡, "the app") is an on-device meal journal and purine intake reference tool.

  • Data controller: Wenbo Zhang (individual developer)
  • Contact email: andyzheung@aliyun.com
  • Support page: https://andyzheung.github.io/sated-legal/support.html
  • Privacy policy: https://andyzheung.github.io/sated-legal/privacy.html

2. Data We Process

This version processes the following data entirely on your device by default:

  • Health profile: daily reference goals, management mode, kidney condition option. Used to display your local purine budget and remaining balance.
  • Meal journal: meal type, food, portion, estimated purine value, timestamp. Used to generate daily summaries, history, and share images.
  • App preferences: local consent record and onboarding status (on-device UserDefaults).
  • Trial state: trial start and expiry dates (on device). Deleting journal data does not restart the trial.
  • Purchase status: subscription or purchase entitlements returned by the App Store. Used to unlock purchased features.
  • Share images: meal summary images you create. Written to your photo library only when you choose to save.

This version does not provide an app account and does not require sign-in. Meal records and health profiles are never uploaded to a developer server.

3. Photos and Camera

This version does not offer food photo recognition or menu scanning. The system add-only photo permission is requested only when you choose to save a share image. The app never reads your photo library.

4. Apple Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your full card number or Apple ID password. The app reads Apple-provided entitlements to unlock Personal or Lifetime features.

Deleting local data does not cancel an Apple subscription. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS subscription settings.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell health data.
  • We do not use ads or tracking.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We do not upload meal records or health profiles to a developer server.
  • We do not provide AI recognition, menu scanning, family sync, family sharing, or reminders (current version).
  • We do not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or risk prediction.

6. System Backup and Sharing

Local data may be affected by iOS backup, device migration, or exports you initiate. Images you save to Photos, send to others, or share into another app are handled by the relevant system feature or third-party service.

7. Deleting Data

You can delete local health profile and meal journal data in the app under Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete Local Data. This does not delete the food reference library and does not cancel Apple subscriptions.

If a future version adds server accounts, AI uploads, or family sync, we will add the relevant deletion process to this policy.

8. Retention

Health profile and meal journal data remain on device until you delete them in the app, uninstall the app, or the system removes app data. Purchase entitlements and subscription records are handled by Apple under its policies. The app never sends health records to a developer server.

9. Health Notice

Purine values, daily goals, and remaining balance are for meal logging and general wellness reference only. Purine content varies by ingredient, cut, preparation, cooking method, and portion size. The app does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or predict medical risk. If you have an acute gout flare, chronic kidney disease, or another medical condition, follow guidance from your clinician or dietitian.

The first-release reference library uses U.S. food samples tested by USDA from the USDA and ODS-NIH Database for the Purine Content of Foods Release 2.0 (2025). Total purines represent four purine bases and exclude separately reported uric acid. Samples have specific raw, cooked, or processed states and coverage is limited. Sources and methods are available in the app.

10. Children

The app is intended for adult wellness logging. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes

If future versions add AI recognition, menu scanning, family sync, accounts, reminders, or cloud services, we will update this policy and explain the new data processing in the app.

12. Contact Us

For questions, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, complaints, or other requests regarding personal information, email andyzheung@aliyun.com. We will handle verified requests in accordance with applicable law.
