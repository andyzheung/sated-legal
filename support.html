常见问题

1. 需要注册账号吗？

不需要。本版本不需要 App 账号，核心记录默认保存在本机。

2. App 会上传我的饮食记录或健康档案吗？

不会。本版本默认不把饮食记录或健康档案上传到开发者服务器。购买状态由 Apple 处理；App 只读取购买权益来解锁功能。

3. 为什么没有 AI 识别、菜单扫描或家庭同步？

这些能力暂不进入 1.0 首发。首发版本只提供手动记录、食物搜索、预算展示、历史记录和分享图片。后续如果上线相关功能，会在版本说明和隐私政策中说明。

4. 如何删除数据？

进入"设置 > 隐私与数据 > 删除本机数据"，可以删除本机健康档案和饮食记录。删除本机数据不会取消 Apple 订阅。

5. 如何取消或管理订阅？

进入"设置 > 购买与订阅 > 管理订阅"，系统会打开 Apple 的订阅管理页面。你也可以在 iOS 的 Apple ID 订阅设置中管理订阅。

6. 嘌呤数值是医疗建议吗？

不是。摄入数值是按每 100g 食物参考值与份量计算的估算，仅用于饮食记录和一般健康参考。数值可能因食材品种、烹调方式、份量和数据差异而变化。痛风急性发作、慢性肾病或其他合并症用户，请遵循医生或营养师建议。

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need an account?

No. This version does not require an app account. Core records stay on your device by default.

2. Does the app upload my meal records or health profile?

No. This version does not upload meal records or health profiles to a developer server. Purchase status is handled by Apple; the app only reads entitlements to unlock features.

3. Why is there no AI recognition, menu scanning, or family sync?

These capabilities are not part of version 1.0. The first release provides manual logging, food search, budget display, history, and share images. Future features will be described in release notes and the privacy policy.

4. How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete Local Data to delete your local health profile and meal journal. Deleting local data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

5. How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Go to Settings > Purchases & Subscriptions > Manage Subscriptions to open Apple's subscription management page. You can also manage subscriptions in your Apple ID settings on iOS.

6. Are the purine values medical advice?

No. Intake values are estimates based on per-100g reference values and portions, for meal logging and general wellness reference only. Values vary by ingredient, preparation, portion, and data differences. If you have an acute gout flare, chronic kidney disease, or another condition, follow guidance from your clinician or dietitian.
